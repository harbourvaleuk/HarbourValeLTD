Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This notice explains how Harbour Vale LTD ("Harbour Vale", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data. We buy products from brand owners, manufacturers and authorised distributors, and sell them online through marketplaces such as Amazon and eBay. We take care to handle personal data lawfully, fairly and openly, in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

In short
  • We collect only what we need to reply to enquiries, work with suppliers and brands, and fulfil marketplace orders.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.
  • This website does not use cookies or analytics tools.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@harbourvale.co.uk.

3. Where we get it from

  • From you, when you complete our contact form, email or call us, or deal with us as a supplier.
  • From marketplaces such as Amazon and eBay, which pass us the limited order and buyer information we need to fulfil and support a sale. We may use this information only for those purposes and as the marketplace's rules allow.
  • From public business sources, such as a brand's own website or business listings, when we contact a company about a possible trading relationship.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where necessary, and we do not sell it. Our recipients include:

  • Service providers acting for us (processors): Formspree, which delivers messages from our contact form to us; GitHub, which hosts this website; Google, which supplies the fonts used on this website; and our email provider.
  • Marketplaces: Amazon and eBay, which operate under their own privacy notices as independent controllers of the data on their platforms.
  • Logistics providers: couriers, freight forwarders and prep or fulfilment partners, if we use them to move or prepare stock.
  • Professional advisers and payment providers: accountants, bookkeepers, lawyers, insurers, banks and payment services.
  • Authorities: HMRC, Companies House, regulators, courts or the police where we are legally required to disclose information.

If we ever sell or restructure our business, personal data may be passed to the new owner, who must continue to protect it under this notice.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers, including Formspree, GitHub and Google, are based in or process data in the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, we rely on a lawful safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge for certified companies) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. You can contact us for more detail on the safeguards used.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including secure connections (HTTPS) on this website, access controls on our accounts and devices, and only sharing data with providers that offer suitable security. No online service can be completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information such as full payment card details by email or through the contact form.

8. Cookies and this website

We do not set cookies on this website, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. When you visit, your browser requests the page from GitHub and the fonts from Google, so those providers receive your IP address and browser details as part of delivering the content. If you use links to other sites, including our Amazon or eBay storefronts, those sites have their own privacy and cookie policies.

9. Business outreach

We may contact brand owners, manufacturers and distributors using business contact details that are publicly available, to ask whether they would like to work with us. Our messages will say who we are. You can tell us at any time that you do not want to be contacted, and we will stop and keep your details on a suppression list so that we do not contact you again. We do not send marketing emails to consumers.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used, as set out in this notice;
  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased in certain circumstances;
  • restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances;
  • receive your data in a portable format, where we use it by consent or contract;
  • object to us using your data based on legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing; and
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@harbourvale.co.uk. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month, and we do not normally charge a fee. Some rights have exceptions, for example where we must keep records by law.
